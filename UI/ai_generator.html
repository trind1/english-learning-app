The Midnight Bakery

translate B1 Intermediate • schedule 3 Min Read

The aroma of fresh bread was ubiquitous in the small village of Oakhaven. Every morning, long before the sun decided to illuminate the cobblestone streets, Elias was already at work. His bakery, a modest wooden structure, was a beacon of warmth in the chilly dawn.

Today was different. Elias had found an ancient recipe book hidden in the walls of the bakery during a recent renovation. The pages were fragile, yellowed with age, and covered in elegant, looping script. He decided to attempt a recipe simply titled "Starlight Loaf."

As he mixed the ingredients, a strange, ethereal glow seemed to emanate from the dough. It wasn't just flour and water; it felt alive. He kneaded it with meticulous care, ensuring every fold was perfect. When he finally placed it into the roaring oven, the fire flickered in unusual hues of blue and silver.

When the loaf emerged, it was a masterpiece. Its crust was dark and crisp, yet it seemed to hold a soft, inner light. The first customer of the day, a skeptical merchant named Silas, took a hesitant bite. Instantly, his eyes widened. He didn't just taste the bread; he experienced a fleeting memory of a joyful childhood summer.

The "Starlight Loaf" was a revelation. It didn't just feed the body; it nourished the soul, leaving everyone who tasted it with a profound sense of peace.
